🔐 Gates & Verification

How our robots prove their homework is really done. No big words inside.

1. Every job comes with a chore card 📋

Every job card has a magic line at the bottom. It says exactly how to check the work. No magic line, no job.

Grown-up words: every bead carries a VERIFY fence, one loop-runnable command. A bead without it is not implementation-ready. br ready only lists beads that have one.

2. Finished? Get a stamp 🔏

Done is not done until a stamp says PASS. Then the locked door opens, but only if you also show the photo of your work (the commit).

Grown-up words: br gate report --status pass records the verdict row. br close --commit-sha needs that PASS row plus a commit whose message cites the bead. No stamp plus photo, no close. Ever.

3. The bouncer at the save button 🦍

When a robot saves work, a bouncer checks three things: name tag on? Did you reserve those crayons? Did your note name the jobs? Miss one, no save.

Grown-up words: the pre-commit guard fails closed on unattributed commits (AGENT_NAME), unreserved paths (toron reservations), and bead tokens in the diff missing from the message. Escape hatches are operator-only.

4. No fake stamps, no skipping the line 🚫

You cannot stamp homework that has no homework. A drawing never gets a swimming stamp. And a suggestion waits in the waiting room until the captain says yes, it never sneaks out early.

Grown-up words: report beads are refused test gates (a unit-test-verified row on docs is a false witness and fails the close). Propose-for-review beads stay open until review lands; that review is their only honest gate.

5. The whole trip 🗺️

Raise your hand, grab your crayons, do the work, run the check, get stamped and save, then shout the news twice, once in the diary, once to the captain.

Grown-up words: claim → reserve → work → VERIFY → gate row → commit-before-close → legal close (durable copy) → [bd-*] done mail (captain's copy). One report, two channels.
